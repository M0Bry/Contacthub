##  About the Project

**ContactHub** is a smart contact management system designed to streamline how you organize and access your important connections. It was developed as a project under the guidance of **Route Academy**.

The application provides an offline-first experience by storing all data in the browser's **Local Storage**, allowing you to manage contacts without an internet connection. It includes full CRUD operations, categorization, favorites, emergency contacts, and a real-time statistics dashboard.

---

##  Features

- **Full CRUD Operations** – Create, read, update, and delete contacts easily.
- **Smart Categorization** – Group contacts into Family, Friends, Work, School, and more.
- **Favorites & Emergency Contacts** – Mark important contacts for quick access.
- **Advanced Search & Filtering** – Search by name, email, or phone number in real time.
- **Rich Contact Profiles** – Store profile picture, phone, email, address, and notes.
- **Statistics Dashboard** – View total contacts, favorites, and emergency contacts at a glance.
- **Offline-First Persistence** – All data is saved in the browser using Local Storage.
- **Responsive Design** – Fully optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **User-Friendly Alerts** – Integrated with SweetAlert2 for elegant popups.
